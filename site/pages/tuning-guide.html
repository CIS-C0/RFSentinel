Fewer false alerts

RF Sentinel errs on the side of warning you. A few minutes of tuning keeps the alerts that matter and silences your own devices, your neighbour's printer and the bus stop's screens. Everything here can be undone.

Where to start

  1. Turn off what you don't need: Settings → What to detect. Each kind of equipment has its own switch. Network / home cameras are off by default because they alert everywhere.
  2. Raise the threshold: Settings → Alerts → "Alert for matches that are at least": Weak alerts on every match, Probable on most, Strong only on near-certain ones. Matches below it still show on the list, flagged, without the alert.
  3. Silence one device or one kind from the list: long-press the row (below).
  4. Space out repeats: Settings → Alerts → "Re-alert the same device after".

Long-press a device

Long-press any row on the list for these. Each one shows an Undo right away.

Its address changes about once a day, so it can't be whitelisted: choose "It's mine" (the app follows it across address changes, best effort), "today only", or pause all tracker follow warnings for 24 h. A whitelisted device still shows on the list by default. To leave them off the list and radar too: Settings → Scanning → Hide trusted devices (they stay in the history).

Changing your mind

  • Whitelist and rules: Settings → Lists, tools & about → Whitelist. "Ignore all like this" rules are under Rules.
  • Network names you whitelisted: Settings → Lists, tools & about → Ignored network names.
  • Ignored trackers: Settings → Trackers & followers → Clear ignored trackers, or long-press the tracker → Stop ignoring this tracker.
  • False alerts: long-press the device → Remove from false alerts, or open History → False alerts.

Follow warnings

Settings → Trackers & followers → "Warn when a device moves with me", for at least a time and over a distance you set. In a car, a short time warns about every device stuck in the same traffic; a longer one (and a longer distance) keeps only what really follows you.

How long devices stay on the list

Settings → Scanning has one slider per source: how long a device stays on the list and radar after it was last heard.

SliderDefaultNotes
Bluetooth30 sDown to 5 s.
WiFi60 sBelow 30 s only with "Wi-Fi scan throttling" off in Android's Developer options.
External hardware60 sDevices heard only by an ESP32 board or a USB WiFi adapter.
Other Sentinels16 minDevices another phone sent over Meshtastic or the internet relay. The sender repeats each one about every 15 minutes, so a shorter time empties the list between repeats. A device this phone hears itself follows its own slider.

Favourite and following devices stay 3 minutes. A flagged device stays as long as it's in range and alerting.

Quieter alerts, same detection

  • Settings → Alerts: alert sound, volume and which audio stream it plays on, vibration (1 pulse weak, 2 probable, 3 strong), and the optional radar-detector beeps.
  • Settings → Voice: what gets spoken, and how short.
  • P25 and pager alerts each have their own Sound / Voice chips in Settings → RTL-SDR radio (RTL-SDR guide).
  • Waze reports: Settings → What to detect → the alert distance, and "Only alert for reports ahead" while you drive.

Q&A

Can I whitelist a police device that's always parked near my home?

Yes, with "Only this device". Avoid "Ignore all like this" for police or emergency equipment: it would also silence every other one, everywhere. The app warns you before saving such a rule.

I whitelisted a phone and it came back. Why?

Most phones change their Bluetooth and WiFi address every few minutes or hours. A new address is a new device. Use a wider rule if one fits (same name, same advert pattern), or leave ordinary phones alone: they don't alert unless they match something.
