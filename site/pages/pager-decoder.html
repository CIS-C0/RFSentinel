POCSAG & FLEX pager decoder for Android

Plug an RTL-SDR or HackRF into the phone and RF Sentinel finds paging channels and decodes the pages, with no computer and no multimon-ng setup. Experimental, off by default.

What it does

  • Decodes POCSAG at 512, 1200 and 2400 baud (pick the speeds) and FLEX into Tools → Pager messages.
  • Alerts on the capcodes (RICs) and words you watch; the page's text stays out of the alert unless you turn that on.
  • Drops the capcodes you ignore.
  • Named channels: switch between them in one tap on the messages screen, and import or export the list.

Turn it on in Settings → RTL-SDR radio → Pager messages. The decoders follow multimon-ng.

Kept in memory only

Pages can hold private or medical details, so they are never saved, exported or sent, their text is never logged, and they are gone when the app closes or you untick the setting.

Check the law

Canada, the United States and many other places restrict using or passing on messages you intercept, and some forbid listening at all. Check the law where you are.

Questions

Can I decode pagers on an Android phone?

Yes, RF Sentinel does it with an RTL-SDR (or HackRF One) on USB OTG. The decoding runs on the phone; no root, no PC, no extra app.

Can one RTL-SDR do pagers and other scans at once?

RF Sentinel takes turns: with the radio sweep, ADS-B, P25 and pagers ticked, the dongle switches between them for the length you set. A second dongle on a powered hub avoids the gaps.
