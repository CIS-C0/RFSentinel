P25 scanner on an Android phone

With an RTL-SDR dongle on USB OTG, RF Sentinel follows a P25 trunked system's control channel, lists the talkgroups and their calls, and can play clear voice through the phone. No root, no computer, no internet stream. Experimental.

P25 control channel

P25 trunked systems (700 / 800 MHz) announce on a control channel which talkgroup gets which voice channel. RF Sentinel finds that channel and reads the announcements. Tools → P25 talkgroups lists each talkgroup with its calls, last channel and radio, and emergency / encrypted flags. Alerts are available for talkgroups you watch, radios nearby, patches and radio GPS positions. On by default in the United States and Canada when an RTL-SDR is plugged in.

Listening to calls

  • Listen to P25 voice (off by default): follows clear calls on the talkgroups you pick and plays them. Encrypted calls are never decoded.
  • Save played calls keeps them to play again; Transcribe P25 calls (Android 13+, off by default) turns each saved call into text with Android's on-device speech recognition: nothing leaves the phone.
  • Play voice transmissions (radio sweep, off by default): a new transmission anywhere in the swept bands is tuned to and played until it goes quiet: analog FM, clear P25, DMR and NXDN48, and FRS / GMRS / MURS walkie-talkies on their real channel.

What you need

  • An RTL-SDR with an R820T / R820T2 / R860 tuner (RTL-SDR Blog V3, V4 or V4L), or a HackRF One (used to receive only).
  • A USB OTG adapter or cable. A powered hub helps with a second dongle.
  • The userspace driver is built in: no driver app, no root.

With more than one SDR scan ticked (radio sweep, ADS-B, P25, pagers) one dongle takes turns. Details on each status line: RTL-SDR guide. How it works inside: hardware internals.

Check the law

Receiving radio signalling is legal in many places but not all, and some restrict using or passing on what you hear. Check the law where you are.

Questions

Can I listen to a P25 police scanner on my Android phone without a computer?

Yes, with an RTL-SDR plugged into the phone. RF Sentinel decodes the control channel and clear P25 voice on the phone itself. Encrypted talkgroups stay silent.

Does it decode DMR and NXDN too?

In RF Sentinel, clear DMR and NXDN48 voice can be played through Play voice transmissions in the radio sweep. Trunking control-channel decoding is P25 only.

Can it decode TETRA?

No. TETRA is encrypted in most networks. RF Sentinel detects TETRA handsets transmitting nearby by their time-slot bursts, without decoding them: see the TETRA detector.
