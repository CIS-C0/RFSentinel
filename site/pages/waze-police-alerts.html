Waze police alerts, louder and sooner

RF Sentinel can pull the police reports Waze users post near you and turn them into real alerts: sound, voice and the car speakers, only for reports on your road ahead, next to what the app hears itself. Optional and off by default.

What you get

  • Police reports, and any other kind you tick: accidents, hazards, road closures, traffic jams. Each kind has its own level: sound and voice, notification only, or silent.
  • What Waze says it is: hiding, mobile speed camera and so on.
  • Alert range from 100 m to 10 km, and a separate map range.
  • Only alert for reports ahead: checked against the map's roads, so a parallel road, a cross street, an overpass or a report behind you stays quiet. While you are guided to a destination, only reports on the part of the route still ahead count.
  • Spoken call-outs at 1 km, 500 m and 200 m as a report gets closer.
  • Reports are scored by distance, age and driver confirmations and re-scored every second as you move, so the alert fires the moment a report comes into range, not at the next check.
  • How often Waze is asked: 15 s to 5 min (direct) or 2 to 10 min (OpenWeb Ninja), slower when parked and faster on fast roads.
  • In the car: alerts through the car speakers with Android Auto, a Waze chip on the live list, and the floating bubble over the Waze app itself.

Why combine both

Waze only knows what other drivers reported. RF Sentinel's police detector hears the equipment itself (body cams, in-car routers, radios), even where nobody reported anything. With both on, one list and one alert voice cover both.

Questions

Can I get louder Waze police alerts on Android?

Yes. With Waze reports on, each police report becomes an RF Sentinel alert: a sound, an optional spoken call-out, a voice boost up to +15 dB, and an option to pause music while it's spoken. It plays through the car speakers too.

Do I need a Waze account?

No. RF Sentinel's Waze direct makes an anonymous account on the phone by itself (stored encrypted). With OpenWeb Ninja, Waze isn't contacted at all.

Can I keep using the Waze app at the same time?

Yes. RF Sentinel runs in the background, and its floating bubble and mini map sit over Waze with a pop-up card saying what was just detected.
