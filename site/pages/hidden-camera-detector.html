Cheap clock, charger and smoke-detector spy cameras give themselves away while they broadcast their setup hotspot, and action and security cameras carry known Bluetooth and WiFi identifiers. RF Sentinel looks for both, and Watch this place tells you what turns up in a room after you arrive.

Action & hidden cameras and Network / home security cameras are off by default (many false alarms at home): turn them on under Settings → detection.

Watch this place: check a hotel room

Settings → Trackers & followers → Watch this place. Leave the phone in one place (home, a hotel room, a rental). It learns every device around for 5 minutes, then, counting only devices heard at -60 dBm or stronger, reports:

  • New: a device it didn't learn, heard twice within a minute.
  • Gone: a device quiet for 2 minutes. Back: one that returns within 5 minutes.
  • Moving: one whose signal keeps jumping by 20 dB or more.

Phones and tags that change their random address count as the same device. It pauses while you are more than 300 m away, and keeps what it learned across a restart. The rules follow AntiHunter's; the numbers can be changed.

Questions

Can I check an Airbnb or hotel room for hidden cameras with my phone?

Partly. In RF Sentinel, turn on the camera categories and Watch this place: cameras broadcasting a setup hotspot, Bluetooth or a known maker's address show up, and anything that switches on after you arrive is reported. Wired, SD-card-only and cellular cameras send nothing a phone can identify: look for lenses and IR LEDs too.

Does it find cameras by lens reflection or infrared?

No. RF Sentinel works only from radio signals. A flashlight lens check or the phone camera (to see IR LEDs) complements it.

Do hidden camera detector apps actually work?

Partly. A phone's magnetometer or camera can't reliably tell a hidden camera from other electronics. RF Sentinel looks for what cameras transmit instead: spy-camera WiFi names and setup networks, and with Watch this place, devices that appear in the room. A camera that only records to a memory card sends nothing, so no radio app can see it; check with a flashlight too.
