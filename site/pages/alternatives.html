RF Sentinel compared with other surveillance-detection tools

There's a small, good community of tools for spotting Flock cameras, police gear and trackers. Here's what each one does, where RF Sentinel fits, and which ones work together. Short version: if you want one free Android app that covers Bluetooth LE, WiFi and cell signals, warns you while you drive, and can also read an OUI-Spy / Flock You ESP32 board, that's RF Sentinel.

Flock You alternative for Android (and how to use both)

Flock You is open-source ESP32 firmware (MIT) dedicated to Flock Safety cameras. Its radio runs in 2.4 GHz promiscuous mode, so it catches Flock cameras' WiFi probe requests and SoftAPs that a phone's WiFi scan can't see, plus Flock battery-pack BLE adverts, and it beeps or feeds a GPS-tagged dashboard.

RF Sentinel brings Flock detection to a phone with no extra hardware: Flock- SSIDs, FS Ext Battery / Penguin- / FS- BLE names, company ID 0x09C8, Raven audio sensors, and the offline OpenStreetMap / DeFlock map with warnings before cellular-only cameras a radio can't hear. It also covers body cams, patrol gear, trackers and drones. Want both? Plug an ESP32 running OUI-Spy's Flock-You mode into the phone: RF Sentinel shows its Flock hits next to its own. OUI-Spy app for Android

OUI-Spy is ESP32 firmware for the XIAO ESP32-S3 with Detector, Flock-You, Foxhunter and Sky Spy modes, a buzzer and LED, and a web flasher. OUI-APEX (lukeswitz's fork) runs those engines in one firmware and pairs with its own app.

RF Sentinel is an Android app that works with OUI-Spy boards: over a USB OTG cable, or wirelessly with OUI-SPY App-Controlled firmware (Settings → Pair OUI-SPY board). It switches the board's Flock, Sky Spy and Detector engines on while scanning and merges their detections with the phone's own (blue ESP32 badge), then adds its rules, patrol-vehicle grouping, alerts, camera map and Android Auto. Wardrive Go alternative: detection instead of mapping

Wardrive Go (GPL-3.0) is an Android wardriving app: live maps of WiFi, Bluetooth and cell networks, WiGLE and WDGWars upload, USB monitor-mode WiFi adapters, channel and spectrum analyzers, Android Auto and Wear OS. It also flags Flock cameras and Axon body cams.

RF Sentinel is built for being warned rather than for mapping: sourced signatures with confidence tiers, evidence fusion, patrol-vehicle grouping, follower alerts, Remote ID decoding, fake-cell signs and warnings before mapped plate, speed and red-light cameras, with spoken alerts in Android Auto. Map for WiGLE with Wardrive Go; drive with RF Sentinel.

Fieldwatch alternative for police and surveillance gear

Fieldwatch (MIT) is a receive-only WiFi and BLE observer for Android with customizable views, a filtering engine, an extensible signature library and reports, fully offline. A great general field-survey tool.

RF Sentinel shares the receive-only, offline approach and is tuned for surveillance and police equipment: alert tiers, patrol-vehicle grouping, tracker follow detection, drone and cell analysis, the camera map and Android Auto.

SØPHIA (s0phia ops) alternative

SØPHIA is a closed-source passive BLE and WiFi recon app for Android sold by DetecX. RF Sentinel does the same kind of recon for free and open source, with police, ALPR, tracker, drone and fake-cell detection on top. Full comparison

Flock-You for Android alternative

Flock-You (Android) is a counter-surveillance app with broad multi-protocol goals (BLE, WiFi, cellular, GNSS, ultrasonic) and Flipper Zero integration. RF Sentinel sticks to what a phone's radios can verify, cites a source for every rule, and connects to ESP32 boards instead of a Flipper.

Bluetooth-only and WiFi-only tools

  • AirGuard (Bluetooth): AirTag / Find My tracker protection. RF Sentinel covers Find My, Google Find Hub, Samsung SmartTag and Tile, with adjustable follow alerts. Tracker guide
  • BLE Radar (MetaRadar) (Bluetooth): BLE environment monitoring with custom filters. RF Sentinel adds WiFi, cell and a sourced police / surveillance rule set.
  • nRF Connect / RF Party (Bluetooth): developer and research BLE scanners.
  • WiFi analyzers (WiFi): access points and channels, no surveillance identification.
  • Rayhunter (cell): IMSI catcher detection on dedicated hardware. RF Sentinel checks the signs Android shows without root. IMSI guide
  • WiGLE WiFi Wardriving: mapping and upload.

Because RF Sentinel listens to Bluetooth LE, WiFi and cell at once, evidence from different radios adds up: a router, a radio and a printer moving together is flagged as a possible police vehicle, something a single-radio tool can't see. How grouping works
