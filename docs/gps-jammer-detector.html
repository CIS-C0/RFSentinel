GPS jammer & spoofing detector

A phone's satellite receiver already reports the strength and position of every satellite it tracks, and on many phones its input noise. RF Sentinel reads that and warns when it looks like a jammer or a spoofer nearby. Nothing is transmitted.

Download APK How detection works

Jamming signs

  • GNSS jamming signs: the receiver's noise (AGC) rises by 10 dB or more while every satellite weakens by 8 dB or more, held for 4 s (60, or 75 from a 15 dB rise). A tunnel or a garage weakens the satellites too but doesn't raise the noise, so it isn't mistaken for jamming. A cheap "GPS blocker" in a vehicle is the usual cause.
  • On phones that don't report AGC, a sudden total loss of every system after a clear sky is only a weak sign.

Spoofing signs

  • Every satellite equally strong whatever its height in the sky (one transmitter), held 15 s, only when the satellites are spread over the sky.
  • Only GPS left while GLONASS, Galileo and BeiDou vanish right after being heard: cheap spoofers fake GPS only.
  • The GPS clock drifting 60 s or more from the network-set clock over 3 fixes.
  • A position jump faster than 340 m/s that the next fixes confirm. Rough fixes are skipped.

Covers every system the phone hears: GPS, GLONASS, Galileo, BeiDou, QZSS, NavIC and SBAS. Tools → Satellites shows each satellite's signal and the receiver noise. On by default; it listens whenever something already uses GPS (the map, a trace, camera warnings or the Satellites screen) and never turns GPS on by itself. These are signs, not proof.

Questions

Can an Android phone detect a GPS jammer?

Often, yes. Phones that report the receiver's AGC let RF Sentinel tell a jammer (noise up, satellites down) from a roof or tunnel (satellites down, noise unchanged). Phones without AGC only get a weak sign on a total loss.

Can it tell if a GPS tracker on my car is being jammed?

RF Sentinel notices jamming around your phone, wherever it comes from: a jammer in your own or a nearby vehicle shows up the same way. To find a tracker itself, see the tracker detector.
