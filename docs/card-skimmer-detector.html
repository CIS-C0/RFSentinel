Bluetooth card skimmer detector

Many gas-pump and ATM skimmers carry a cheap Bluetooth serial module so the thief can download stolen card data from the car park. Those modules announce themselves with factory names. RF Sentinel flags them while it scans.

Download APK How detection works

What it looks for

SignScore
A Bluetooth name starting with HC-03, HC-05, HC-06, RN42 or BT04-A70 (probable)
The Serial Port Profile service (0x1101) with no telling name50 (probable, low end)

The Card skimmers category is on by default. Signatures follow SquachWatch's detection list, re-checked against the Bluetooth SIG and IEEE registries.

At the pump

  1. Open the app while you pull up; it scans in the background.
  2. A Possible card skimmer alert near the pump means a serial module is broadcasting close by. Tap it → Locate: the beeps speed up as you get closer.
  3. If it peaks at a pump, pay inside or use another one, and tell the station.

Questions

Can my phone detect a credit card skimmer?

A Bluetooth one, often: RF Sentinel flags its serial module. Skimmers that store data on a chip without a radio, or send it over a cellular modem, broadcast nothing a phone can identify: also check the card slot and keypad by hand.

Can an HC-05 alert be something harmless?

Yes. The same modules are used in hobby electronics, some car gadgets and industrial gear. That's why RF Sentinel rates it a probable match, not a strong one: what matters is where the signal peaks.

How can I tell if a gas pump has a Bluetooth skimmer?

Open RF Sentinel at the pump: Bluetooth skimmers often use cheap serial modules (HC-05 / HC-06 style) whose names RF Sentinel flags. Also check the panel seal and the card reader. No alert doesn't prove a pump is clean, because many skimmers have no radio.

Is there an app like Bluetana?

Bluetana was a university research app for finding Bluetooth skimmers at gas pumps. RF Sentinel does a similar check for anyone, free and open source: it flags the Bluetooth module names skimmers commonly use, along with its other detections.
