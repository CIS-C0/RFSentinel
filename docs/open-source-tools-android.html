rf_sentinel / guides / open-source-tools-android

rtl_433, dump1090, multimon-ng, SDRTrunk and more on Android

RF Sentinel stands on the work of many open-source projects. This page lists each one, what it is, what RF Sentinel took from it (ported code, a reference, or only an idea) and what you get on an Android phone. Short version: RF Sentinel runs parts of rtl_433, dump1090, multimon-ng, DSD, OP25 and SDRTrunk's work directly on the phone with an RTL-SDR, no root and no computer, but it does only the parts it needs for detection. For everything those tools do, use them on a computer.

rtl_433 on Android: tire sensors

rtl_433 decodes hundreds of kinds of 315, 433 and 868 MHz devices: weather stations, doorbells, car keys and tire-pressure sensors. It runs on a computer or Raspberry Pi.

RF Sentinel ports only rtl_433's tire-pressure (TPMS) decoders, and runs them on the phone with an RTL-SDR: Ford, Toyota (PMV-C210 and PMV-107J), Citroen / Peugeot, Hyundai (VDO) and Schrader (GM / Chrysler, Saab / Opel, Subaru, Nissan / Renault, BMW). The goal isn't to read tire pressure. It's to notice the same car's wheels turning up with you at place after place, a car following you even when its driver's phone is off. Your own car is learned first and never alerts. See car-following detection.

dump1090 on Android: ADS-B without internet

dump1090 and FlightAware's dump1090-fa decode the 1090 MHz ADS-B messages aircraft send. RF Sentinel's ADS-B decoder was written from the published format and Junzi Sun's The 1090 MHz Riddle, with SkyPulse and dump1090 as references; its 2.4 MS/s demodulator and automatic gain are ports of dump1090-fa's (Oliver Jowett and FlightAware).

With an RTL-SDR on the phone, RF Sentinel shows the aircraft around you on its map with no internet, and warns about police helicopters and planes circling low. For a 24/7 feeder station, dump1090-fa on a Raspberry Pi is still the tool.

multimon-ng on Android: POCSAG and FLEX pagers

multimon-ng decodes many digital modes from audio. RF Sentinel's POCSAG (512, 1200, 2400 baud) and FLEX decoders follow it, and run straight from the RTL-SDR's samples on the phone: no audio piping, no computer. RF Sentinel keeps pages in memory only and alerts on the capcodes and words you watch. See the pager decoder.

DSD, mbelib, dsd-neo and DSD-FME on Android: digital voice

RF Sentinel's P25 voice is ported from DSD and mbelib (szechyjs), with mbelib's AMBE+2 for DMR and NXDN. DMR voice is ported from dsd-neo by arancormonk and NXDN48 from DSD-FME by lwvmobile.

On the phone, RF Sentinel plays clear (unencrypted) P25 Phase 1 calls it follows from the control channel, and clear DMR and NXDN48 voice through Play voice transmissions in the radio sweep. Encrypted calls are never decoded, and P25 Phase 2 isn't supported. See the P25 scanner.

SDRTrunk and OP25 on a phone

SDRTrunk (Dennis Sheirer) and OP25 are full trunking decoders for a computer. RF Sentinel checked its P25 fields, Reed-Solomon and NID decoding against SDRTrunk, follows SDRTrunk's auto-PPM frequency correction and its MDC-1200 decoder (unit IDs and emergencies on analog FM radios), and takes its P25 call-follow timings from dsd-neo and OP25.

RF Sentinel is narrower: one P25 Phase 1 system at a time, built around "is someone transmitting near me?" rather than monitoring a whole county. If you run a scanner station, keep SDRTrunk. If you want P25 talkgroups and clear calls in your pocket or your car, RF Sentinel does it on the phone.

RFAnalyzer

RFAnalyzer by Dennis Mantz is an Android spectrum analyzer and receiver for HackRF and RTL-SDR. RF Sentinel's waterfall Listen follows its demodulator. RFAnalyzer is the better general-purpose receiver; RF Sentinel's waterfall is a tool around its detections.

rtl-sdr-blog librtlsdr

The RTL-SDR Blog driver is where RF Sentinel's bias-tee switch comes from. RF Sentinel drives RTL-SDR Blog V3, V4 and V4L, Nooelec NESDR and most generic dongles with its own USB driver, so no driver app and no root are needed.

AIMSICD, SnoopSnitch and Rayhunter

AIMSICD and SnoopSnitch are Android fake-cell (IMSI catcher) detectors. Two of RF Sentinel's fake-cell checks follow their ideas; no code was taken. SnoopSnitch's deeper checks need a rooted phone with a Qualcomm chip; RF Sentinel uses only what non-rooted Android shows.

EFF's Rayhunter runs on a cheap mobile hotspot and reads the modem's signalling directly, which a normal phone can't. RF Sentinel's 2G-downgrade check is the visible side of what Rayhunter watches; running both is reasonable. See IMSI catcher detector and RF Sentinel vs Rayhunter.

AirGuard alternative

AirGuard by SEEMOO / TU Darmstadt is a focused, research-backed Android tracker detector. RF Sentinel's tracker service IDs follow AirGuard's. AirGuard is the simpler choice if trackers are all you want; RF Sentinel adds trackers to police, camera, drone and fake-cell detection in one app, a Locate screen to find the tag, and car-following detection from tire sensors. See tracker detection.

Chasing Your Tail on Android

Chasing Your Tail (NG) looks for devices that keep turning up around you, from Kismet logs on a computer. RF Sentinel's "keeps turning up" follower check follows it on the phone, with no Kismet or Raspberry Pi: a tracker or flagged device that turns up at 3 or more places along your way within 2 hours raises may be following you.

AntiHunter on Android

AntiHunter is an ESP32 project for watching an area for devices. RF Sentinel's Watch this place follows its rules: it learns the place for 5 minutes, then reports what's new, gone, back or moved, on the phone. See Watch this place.

OpenDroneID receiver

OpenDroneID's receiver-android is the reference Remote ID app. RF Sentinel's Remote ID over Wi-Fi Aware follows it, and adds Bluetooth and WiFi beacon Remote ID, overhead alerts and the pilot's position on its map. See drone detector.

FlipDeFlock

FlipDeFlock spots Flock cameras on a Flipper Zero from the shape of their WiFi probe requests. RF Sentinel ports its probe-shape hash so the values match, with its candidate and generic shape lists, and uses it with a USB WiFi adapter on the phone. See Flock camera detector.

Momentum BLE Spam

RF Sentinel names Google Fast Pair devices from the genuine-device list in Momentum-Apps' BLE Spam. RF Sentinel also flags Bluetooth spam floods and Flipper Zero devices nearby: see Flipper Zero detector.

V2X2MAP

An ESP32-C5 running V2X2MAP by Peter Holzhauser hears European 5.9 GHz vehicle-to-everything (ITS-G5) messages. RF Sentinel reads its frames the same way and decodes the CAM / DENM messages from ETSI's ASN.1, so vehicles and road warnings show on the phone's map.

WiFi monitor mode on Android without root

On Linux, adapters like the ALFA AWUS036ACH use kernel drivers such as aircrack-ng/rtl8812au, which Android can't load without root. RF Sentinel ships its own receive-only USB drivers instead: the Realtek 88xxau chips (RTL8811AU, RTL8821AU, RTL8812AU), RTL8814AU, MT7612U, RT3070 and AR9271 ported from Wardrive Go (register tables from Realtek's Linux driver), RTL8812BU / RTL8822BU from OpenIPC's devourer, MT7610U and MT7921AU following the Linux mt76 driver, and RTL8187 with Kismet's Android PCAP Capture as reference. Every transmit path and handshake capture was removed. See hardware.

DeFlock

DeFlock maps plate-reader cameras on OpenStreetMap with volunteers. RF Sentinel ships a snapshot of those cameras with OpenStreetMap's speed and red-light cameras, and warns before each one offline, on the phone and on Android Auto. If you add cameras, add them to OpenStreetMap through DeFlock; every RF Sentinel user benefits on the next update. See camera alerts.

Inspiration

RF Sentinel was inspired by the nyanBOX hardware device and the RF Party app, which came from Alan Meekins' DEF CON 31 talk Snoop Unto Them As They Snoop Unto Us. Feature ideas also came from SØPHIA, BLE Radar (MetaRadar) and ESPsoup; no code from those projects is included. Thank you to every project here.

Questions

Is there an rtl_433 app for Android?

RF Sentinel runs rtl_433's tire-pressure sensor decoders on an Android phone with an RTL-SDR, no root and no computer, to spot cars following you. It doesn't decode rtl_433's other devices (weather stations, doorbells); for those, run rtl_433 on a computer or Raspberry Pi.

Can I run dump1090 on an Android phone?

RF Sentinel includes a port of dump1090-fa's demodulator and automatic gain, so an RTL-SDR on the phone decodes ADS-B with no internet and shows aircraft on RF Sentinel's map, with alerts for police aircraft circling low. It isn't a feeder for flight-tracking sites.

Is there multimon-ng for Android?

For pagers, yes: RF Sentinel's POCSAG and FLEX decoders follow multimon-ng and run on the phone from an RTL-SDR or HackRF. RF Sentinel doesn't do multimon-ng's other modes (DTMF, EAS, AFSK and so on).

Does SDRTrunk run on Android?

SDRTrunk is a desktop Java application. On a phone, RF Sentinel decodes a P25 Phase 1 control channel, shows active talkgroups and plays clear calls with an RTL-SDR, using checks taken from SDRTrunk. It's narrower than SDRTrunk: one system at a time and no Phase 2.

Can I use OP25 or DSD on a phone?

RF Sentinel ports DSD and mbelib's P25 voice, dsd-neo's DMR voice and DSD-FME's NXDN48 voice to Android, so clear digital voice plays on the phone with an RTL-SDR and no computer. Encrypted calls are never decoded.

Is there a police scanner app that works without internet?

Streaming scanner apps need internet. RF Sentinel with an RTL-SDR receives the radio itself, offline: it shows nearby transmissions, P25 talkgroups and, if you turn it on, plays clear analog, P25, DMR and NXDN voice. Check your local law before listening.

Can I use an RTL-SDR on Android without root or a driver app?

Yes. RF Sentinel has its own USB driver for RTL-SDR dongles (RTL-SDR Blog V3, V4 and V4L, Nooelec and most generic ones) and HackRF One, so it needs no root and no separate driver app. An OTG adapter is all you need; weak phones may need a powered hub.

Does the RTL-SDR Blog V4 work on Android?

Yes, RF Sentinel supports the RTL-SDR Blog V4 and V4L (R828D tuner, 28.8 MHz crystal, its band inputs and notch filters), including its bias-tee. If the phone doesn't see it, try another OTG adapter, a shorter cable or a powered hub.

Can an RTL8812AU or ALFA adapter do monitor mode on Android without root?

Yes, with RF Sentinel. Its own receive-only USB drivers put RTL8812AU, RTL8812BU, RTL8814AU, MT7612U, MT7921AU, RT3070, AR9271 and RTL8187 adapters in monitor mode on a non-rooted phone, to hear probe requests and every network. They never transmit.

What is a good AirGuard alternative?

RF Sentinel uses the same tracker IDs as AirGuard (AirTag, SmartTag, Tile, Chipolo, Find Hub tags) and adds a Locate screen, car-following detection from tire sensors, and police, camera, drone and fake-cell detection in the same free, open-source app. If you only want tracker alerts, AirGuard is a fine, focused choice.

Is there Chasing Your Tail for Android?

RF Sentinel's follower check is modelled on Chasing Your Tail: a tracker or flagged device that turns up at 3 or more places along your way within 2 hours raises "may be following you". It runs on the phone, with no Kismet or Raspberry Pi.

Can my phone do what AntiHunter does?

RF Sentinel's Watch this place follows AntiHunter's rules on an Android phone: leave the phone in a room or a parked car and it reports the devices that arrive, leave, come back or move. For a fixed perimeter with many sensors, AntiHunter's ESP32 nodes do more.

Is there a SnoopSnitch or AIMSICD alternative that works without root?

RF Sentinel checks for fake cell tower signs on a non-rooted Android phone (test networks, sudden 2G downgrades, cloned cell IDs, area codes no neighbour shares), with two checks following AIMSICD and SnoopSnitch ideas. Without root it can't see signalling; for that, use EFF's Rayhunter.

Can I detect Flock cameras like FlipDeFlock but on a phone?

Yes. RF Sentinel uses FlipDeFlock's probe-shape hash with a USB WiFi adapter on the phone, and also finds Flock cameras from their WiFi and Bluetooth with the phone alone, plus offline alerts before every mapped plate reader.

Is there an app like DeFlock that warns me while I drive?

RF Sentinel ships DeFlock's OpenStreetMap snapshot of plate-reader cameras and warns about 20 seconds before each one, offline, on the phone and on Android Auto. It also detects cameras that aren't mapped yet from their radio signals.

Is RF Sentinel allowed to use this code?

Yes. RF Sentinel is GPL-3.0, and every ported project's licence (GPL, ISC, BSD, MIT, Apache-2.0) allows it. RF Sentinel keeps their notices in the source (sdr/NOTICE.md, usb/NOTICE.md) and credits each project in its README.
